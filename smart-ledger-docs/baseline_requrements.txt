# SmartSilk — System Requirements & Scope

**Project:** SmartSilk
**Stage:** Step 3 — Define System Requirements and Scope
**Project Type:** Semester Project
**Domain:** Silk Yarn Trading and Warp Manufacturing
**Primary User:** Business Owner / Admin

---

# 1. Purpose of the System

SmartSilk is a business management system designed for a family-run silk yarn trading and warp manufacturing business.

The purpose of SmartSilk is not merely to digitize existing notebooks and ledgers. The system is intended to provide a consolidated and reliable view of:

* Raw silk availability
* Material movement
* Customer orders
* Production and work-in-progress
* Outsourced manufacturing
* Customer receivables
* Supplier payables
* Worker obligations
* Cash and bank balances
* Expenses
* Personal drawings
* Loans
* Chit investments
* Bills and business records
* Upcoming financial commitments
* Business performance

The system should help the business owner make decisions using recorded business information instead of relying primarily on memory, notebooks, and mental calculations.

---

# 2. System Objectives

SmartSilk has the following major objectives:

1. Digitize the important operational and financial records of the business.
2. Provide visibility into the current physical material position.
3. Track customer orders and outstanding amounts.
4. Track supplier purchases, credit and outstanding payables.
5. Track in-house and outsourced warp production.
6. Track worker and manufacturer obligations.
7. Maintain a reliable financial transaction history.
8. Distinguish actual available money from receivables, payables and future commitments.
9. Track physical cash and multiple business bank accounts.
10. Maintain historical records for audit and correction purposes.
11. Provide reminders for important business obligations.
12. Provide a consolidated owner dashboard.
13. Establish a foundation for future AI-assisted decision support.
14. Improve business visibility without unnecessarily complicating the system.

# 5. Functional Requirements

## 5.1 Supplier and Purchase Management

SmartSilk shall:

* Maintain supplier records.
* Record raw silk purchases.
* Record purchase date.
* Record raw silk weight in kilograms.
* Record purchase amount.
* Record supplier credit terms.
* Calculate or record supplier due dates.
* Track supplier outstanding amounts.
* Track overdue supplier obligations.
* Support supplier-specific credit periods.
* Support configurable supplier penalties where applicable.
* Record supplier payments.
* Allocate supplier payments to the oldest outstanding purchase first.
* Preserve supplier payment history.
* Attach purchase documents where available.
* Track whether purchased material has been received.

### Purchase triggers

Purchasing may be initiated because of:

* Confirmed customer demand
* Raw silk trading demand
* Low stock
* Expected future demand

The system may provide a low-stock indication, but purchasing remains an owner decision.

### Purchase pricing

The owner manually enters:

* Weight
* Price per kilogram
* Total purchase amount

The system must not attempt to automatically predict daily silk prices.

# 6. Inventory and Material Flow

SmartSilk shall provide visibility into the physical location and state of business material.

## 6.1 Main inventory categories

The system shall distinguish:

* Available raw silk
* Allocated raw silk
* Raw silk in processing
* Rolling WIP
* Warp-production WIP
* Outsourced WIP
* Finished warp
* Returned material
* Wastage
* Material discrepancies

The primary quantity measurement is:

> **Weight in kilograms.**

Detailed warp measurements such as length, width, thread count and similar specifications are outside the current scope.

## 6.2 Material destinations

Purchased raw silk may be:

* Sold directly as raw silk
* Used for in-house production
* Sent for outsourced production

## 6.3 Vuda Warp

Vuda warp is treated as a directly traded product.

The system shall support:

```text
Purchase Vuda Warp
       ↓
Inventory
       ↓
Sale
```

No production workflow is required for Vuda warp.

## 6.4 Wastage

The system shall allow the Admin to manually record wastage.

The system shall not attempt to automatically determine wastage.

This allows the system to distinguish:

```text
Available Material
WIP
Finished Material
Wastage
```

## 6.5 Returns

Returned material shall be represented as a business event.

If returned material is usable:

```text
Return
  ↓
Inventory
  ↓
Possible resale
```

If unusable:

```text
Return
  ↓
Wastage
```

If the customer is responsible for the problem and the return is rejected, the customer remains responsible for the payment.

# 7. Customer and Sales Order Management

SmartSilk shall:

* Maintain customer records.
* Record customer orders.
* Record order dates.
* Record product type.
* Record weight.
* Record selling price.
* Record total order value.
* Record advance payments.
* Record outstanding amount.
* Track order status.
* Track delivery.
* Associate orders with production where applicable.
* Track returns or damage where applicable.

Customers may purchase:

* Raw silk
* Kora / normal warp
* Silk warp
* Vuda warp

## 7.1 Customer credit

Customers do not have one universal fixed credit period.

Customers may:

* Pay in advance
* Pay partially
* Pay after receiving material
* Pay after several months
* Pay the complete amount later

Therefore SmartSilk shall not create artificial fixed due dates for customers.

Instead, the system shall track:

* Outstanding amount
* Order date
* Age of outstanding amount

Customer-specific credit periods may be configured where required.

## 7.2 Payment allocation

Customer payments shall normally follow:

> **Oldest unpaid order first.**

A single payment may settle multiple orders.

An order may receive multiple payments.

The system shall therefore maintain order-level and customer-level outstanding balances.

---

# 8. In-house Production and Worker Management

The primary internally produced warp is:

> **Kora / Normal Warp**

## 8.1 Production flow

```text
Raw Silk
   ↓
Rolling
   ↓
Rolls
   ↓
Warping
   ↓
Finished Kora Warp
```

Production may be triggered by:

* Customer orders
* Anticipated demand during busy periods

## 8.2 Production tracking

SmartSilk shall record:

* Warp type
* Weight
* Production date
* Worker
* Production status
* Order association where applicable
* Finished production
* Wastage where manually identified

Weight is the primary production measurement.

## 8.3 Rolling workers

Rolling workers are generally paid according to:

```text
Hours worked × applicable rate
```

Payments are generally made weekly.

## 8.4 Warp workers

Warp workers are generally paid according to:

```text
Warp weight × applicable rate
```

Payments are generally made weekly.

Worker rates are mostly fixed and may change annually.

Historical work must retain the rate applicable when that work was performed.

## 8.5 Production allocation

The owner decides which customer receives completed warp.

The normal priority may be first-come-first-served, but urgent requirements may change the allocation.

---

# 9. Outsourced Warp / Job Work

SmartSilk shall track warp manufacturing performed by external manufacturers.

## 9.1 Reasons for outsourcing

Outsourcing may occur because:

* Customer requires a particular warp
* Internal workers are unavailable
* Internal production capacity is occupied
* External machine capacity is available
* Additional production capacity is required

## 9.2 Outsourcing tracking

The system shall record:

* Manufacturer
* Date material was supplied
* Raw silk weight supplied
* Warp type
* Related customer/order where applicable
* Current WIP
* Finished warp received
* Finished weight
* Applicable production rate
* Amount payable
* Payment status
* Payment history

## 9.3 Manufacturer payment

Manufacturer payment is generally calculated based on:

```text
Finished warp weight × applicable rate
```

Rates may vary by warp type.

Payments are generally settled monthly.

## 9.4 Outsourced wastage

The business currently does not systematically receive or monitor manufacturer wastage.

SmartSilk shall therefore track the material supplied and finished material received, while allowing the Admin to manually identify and record discrepancies or wastage where required.

## 9.5 Outsourcing analysis

The system should provide a foundation for comparing:

* In-house production cost
* Outsourcing cost
* Material cost
* Customer revenue
* Contribution/profitability

Advanced profitability analysis may be expanded later.

---

# 10. Customer Payments and Receivables

SmartSilk shall:

* Record customer payments.
* Record payment date.
* Record payment amount.
* Associate payment with customer.
* Allocate payments to outstanding orders.
* Support partial payments.
* Support multiple payments for an order.
* Maintain customer outstanding balance.
* Maintain order-level payment status.
* Preserve payment history.

Payment allocation is normally:

> Oldest unpaid order first.

Customer payments reduce receivables and increase the relevant cash/bank position.

# 11. Financial Ledger, Bank and Cash Management

SmartSilk shall maintain a central financial record while preserving business-specific views.

## 11.1 Financial transaction principle

Every meaningful money movement shall be traceable to:

* Transaction type
* Business reason
* Source
* Destination where applicable
* Date
* Amount

The system must distinguish:

> **Business events from money movements.**

For example:

```text
Sale → creates customer receivable
Payment → settles receivable and moves money
```

## 11.2 Payment methods

General financial transactions may use:

* Cash
* UPI
* Bank transfer
* Cheque

Chit transactions are an exception and are **cash-only**.

## 11.3 Bank accounts

The system shall support:

* Multiple business bank accounts
* Manual account configuration
* Opening balance
* Current balance
* Transaction history
* Bank-specific transactions

## 11.4 Physical cash

Physical business cash shall be tracked separately.

Cash may enter through:

* Customer cash payments
* Bank withdrawals
* Refunds/returned money

Cash may leave through:

* Expenses
* Supplier payments
* Worker payments
* Chit contributions
* Loan payments
* Personal drawings

## 11.5 Internal transfers

The system shall support:

* Bank → Cash
* Cash → Bank
* Bank → Bank

Internal transfers are not revenue or expenses.

## 11.6 Available money

The system shall define available money as:

```text
Available Money
=
Physical Cash
+
Available/Cleared Bank Balances
```

Customer receivables and future financial inflows shall not be treated as currently available money.

## 11.7 Cheques

Cheques shall have a pending/cleared state.

A pending cheque shall not affect actual available balance until clearance.

## 11.8 Reconciliation

The system shall support:

* Identification of cash/bank discrepancies
* Manual reconciliation
* Documented balance adjustments
* Reason for adjustments
* Preservation of previous history

The system shall not silently overwrite historical balances.

## 11.9 Bank integration

For the MVP, bank transactions may be entered manually.

Future functionality may include:

* Bank API integration
* Automatic transaction retrieval
* Automatic bank deductions/EMI detection
* Statement import
* Automated reconciliation

Manual entry must remain available even when integrations exist.

---

# 12. Expenses and Personal Drawings

SmartSilk shall record business expenses.

Each expense shall support:

* Date
* Amount
* Category/reason
* Payment source
* Recurring or one-time classification
* Optional supporting document

Expense categories shall be configurable.

The expense date represents the actual payment date.

## 12.1 Personal drawings

When business money is used for personal purposes, it shall be classified separately as:

> **Personal Drawing**

Personal drawings shall not automatically be treated as ordinary operating expenses in profitability analysis.

## 12.2 Refunds

Refunds shall be recorded separately from the original expense.

Where applicable, available refund adjustments shall be tracked and may later be applied against another customer order.

---

# 13. Chit Management

SmartSilk shall manage multiple chit investments.

Each chit shall maintain a separate history.

## 13.1 Chit contributions

Chit contributions are:

> **Physical cash only.**

The system shall record:

* Chit
* Contribution date
* Contribution amount
* Contribution history
* Remaining expected contributions

Monthly contribution amounts may change based on the chit bidding process.

## 13.2 Chit bidding

The chit supports an auction/bidding mechanism.

Conceptually:

```text
Participant bids
      ↓
Bid deduction
      ↓
Payout amount
      ↓
Benefit/deduction distributed
      ↓
Future contribution changes
```

The exact chit-specific calculation formula is intentionally left as a placeholder:

> **CHIT_BIDDING_CALCULATION**

The exact formula will be implemented after the business-specific calculation is finalized.

## 13.3 Chit payout

Chit payout is:

> **Physical cash only.**

The system shall record:

* Chit
* Payout date
* Payout amount

The purpose or subsequent use of the payout does not need to be recorded.

## 13.4 Organiser payment

Additional organiser payment shall be recorded separately.

## 13.5 Business classification

The business treats the chit as an investment.

Chit contributions and payouts must remain distinguishable from normal sales revenue and ordinary operating expenses.

The business's chosen analysis may treat the lump-sum benefit as profit/investment return.

---

# 14. Loan Management

SmartSilk shall support two loan categories:

1. Bank loans
2. Private-person loans

## 14.1 Loan information

The system shall record:

* Loan type
* Lender
* Original principal
* Current principal outstanding
* Interest information
* Interest due
* Payment history
* Loan status

Loan purpose is not required.

## 14.2 Interest

Interest and principal shall be tracked separately.

For private loans:

* Interest can be manually recorded.
* Interest may be paid separately from principal.

For bank loans:

* Interest information may be entered manually in the MVP.
* Future bank integration may provide relevant interest/EMI information automatically.

## 14.3 Principal repayment

The system shall support:

* Partial principal repayment
* Full repayment
* Early repayment

Early repayment has:

> **No penalty according to the current business requirement.**

## 14.4 Loan completion

A loan is completed when its required obligations have been settled.

Completed loans shall remain in historical records.

## 14.5 Loan decision support

SmartSilk should eventually suggest whether available money should be used for:

* Interest payment
* Partial principal repayment
* Full repayment

The recommendation shall consider:

* Available cash/bank balance
* Supplier obligations
* Worker obligations
* Chit commitments
* Other immediate financial requirements

The system only provides a recommendation.

It shall never automatically execute the payment.

---

# 15. Bills, Documents and Audit Records

SmartSilk shall retain business documents associated with relevant records.

## 15.1 Sales bills

A sales bill may normally be generated after the order is fully paid.

However:

> A bill may be generated before full payment when requested by the customer.

A bill therefore does not automatically mean that the order is paid.

Bills shall have unique identifiers/numbers.

## 15.2 Purchase documents

Purchase bills/documents should be associated with supplier purchases.

Supplier bills received digitally may eventually support automated extraction.

## 15.3 Supporting documents

Expense receipts and supporting documents are optional.

The business does not require a document for every expense.

## 15.4 Audit retrieval

The system should allow records to be retrieved by:

* Date/period
* Transaction type
* Business category

Records may be downloaded for audit purposes.

## 15.5 History preservation

Issued documents and important transactions must not be silently deleted or overwritten.

Corrections should preserve the original history.

## 15.6 GST

Detailed GST functionality is **outside the current MVP scope**.

Future GST functionality may include:

* GST calculation
* HSN/SAC
* CGST/SGST/IGST
* GST reconciliation
* Credit/debit notes
* Advanced GST reporting

# 16. Notifications, Reminders and Alerts

SmartSilk shall provide configurable reminders for important business conditions.

## 16.1 Supplier reminders

The system shall support:

* Reminder before due date
* Due-date notification
* Overdue notification
* Total supplier payable
* Individual purchase payable

Default reminder timing may be approximately 3–4 days before due date and must be configurable.

---

## 16.2 Customer reminders

The system may notify when:

* Customer credit period is approaching
* Outstanding becomes due according to customer-specific terms
* Outstanding becomes overdue/significant

Default reminder timing may be approximately 1–2 days before the relevant period and must be configurable.

---

## 16.3 Loan reminders

The system shall support:

* Upcoming loan payment reminders
* Interest reminders
* Loan identification
* Amount due

The timing must be configurable.

---

## 16.4 Chit reminders

The system shall notify which chit requires payment.

The notification does not need to display the amount.

The user can open the chit module to see the amount and calculation.

---

## 16.5 Worker payments

Worker payments are displayed within the system.

No notification is required.

---

## 16.6 Outsourcing

No notification is required for outsourced work because the business currently handles this operationally through phone communication.

---

## 16.7 Raw material alerts

The system shall alert when:

* Raw silk falls below its configured threshold.
* Available raw silk is insufficient for confirmed orders.

The alert is decision support, not automatic purchasing.

---

## 16.8 Finished warp

No automatic notification is required when finished warp is completed.

The business physically receives, checks and weighs the finished warp before dispatch.

---

## 16.9 Financial alerts

The system should identify conditions such as:

* Low available cash
* Large upcoming supplier obligations
* Large upcoming loan obligations
* Chit commitments
* Insufficient money for known obligations
* Bank/cash mismatch
* Significant customer outstanding

---

## 16.10 Notification configuration

The Admin shall be able to configure:

* Notification category
* Enabled/disabled state
* Timing
* Frequency
* Priority

Notifications remain unresolved until the underlying condition is resolved.

# 19. Global Business Rules

The following rules apply across the system.

## BR-01 — Admin control

Only the Admin can modify SmartSilk records in the MVP.

## BR-02 — Historical preservation

Important historical records must not be silently deleted or overwritten.

## BR-03 — Corrections

Incorrect financial or important business records should be corrected through reversal, adjustment or correction mechanisms that preserve history.

## BR-04 — Business event vs money movement

A business event and the associated money movement are separate concepts.

Examples:

```text
Purchase → Supplier payable
Payment → Money movement
Sale → Customer receivable
Customer payment → Money movement
Loan → Liability + money received
```

## BR-05 — Internal transfers

Transfers between the business's own financial locations are not revenue or expenses.

## BR-06 — Payment allocation

Customer and supplier payments normally follow oldest-outstanding-first allocation.

## BR-07 — Available money

Available money is based on physical cash and available/cleared bank balances.

Receivables and future expected money are not immediately available money.

## BR-08 — Personal drawings

Business money used personally must remain distinguishable from ordinary business expenses.

## BR-09 — Chit payment method

Chit contributions and payouts are cash-only.

## BR-10 — Loan classification

Loan principal and interest must be distinguishable.

Principal repayment is not an ordinary operating expense.

## BR-11 — Inventory measurement

Weight in kilograms is the primary material measurement for the current SmartSilk system.

## BR-12 — Manual wastage

Wastage is recorded manually by the Admin.

## BR-13 — Manual pricing

The owner controls purchase and selling prices.

The system does not automatically predict daily silk prices.

## BR-14 — Owner decision authority

The system may provide alerts, calculations and recommendations, but important business decisions remain with the owner.

## BR-15 — AI safety

AI may extract or suggest information, but it must not directly modify critical financial records without owner confirmation.

## BR-16 — Documents are supporting records

Transactions remain the source of truth. Documents represent or support those transactions.

## BR-17 — Opening balances

Existing business state is manually initialized when SmartSilk is first introduced.

# 20. Non-Functional Requirements

## 20.1 Reliability

Financial and material records must remain consistent after successful transactions.

## 20.2 Data integrity

Financial operations involving balances, payments and obligations must maintain transactional consistency.

## 20.3 Security

* Authentication is required.
* Only the Admin can access the system in the MVP.
* Financial modifications must be protected.
* Sensitive business information must not be exposed unnecessarily.

## 20.4 Auditability

Important transactions should have:

* Timestamp
* Historical state
* Modification/correction history where applicable

## 20.5 Usability

The system should minimize manual complexity because the primary user is a business owner who currently relies heavily on quick ledger entries and mental calculations.

Important information should be accessible with minimal navigation.

## 20.6 Maintainability

The system should use a modular architecture so that future features can be added without redesigning the entire application.

## 20.7 Performance

Normal business operations such as:

* Recording payments
* Recording orders
* Checking inventory
* Viewing outstanding balances
* Viewing the dashboard

should respond quickly under expected small-business workloads.

## 20.8 Scalability

The MVP does not require enterprise-scale distributed architecture.

A modular monolithic application is sufficient for the current business size.
