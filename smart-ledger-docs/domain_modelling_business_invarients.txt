Step 4.6 — Define Domain Invariants & Business Rules

A domain invariant is a rule that must always remain true in SmartSilk.

For example:

A chit payment can only be recorded as physical cash.

This isn't just a UI preference—it is a business rule.

4.6.1 Purchase & Raw Material Rules
Rule 1 — Raw silk is weight-based

SmartSilk's raw-silk inventory is measured primarily in:

kg

Length, thread count, dimensions, etc. are outside the current scope.

Rule 2 — Purchase price is manually determined

The owner enters:

Total purchase amount
Total weight
Price per kg

The system can calculate:

Price per kg = Total amount / Total weight

Rule 3 — Purchase price is historical

The system must preserve the actual purchase price of each purchase.

It must not recalculate old purchases using today's silk price.

Rule 4 — Purchase receipt can be partial

Although normally an entire purchased package/bundle arrives together, SmartSilk must allow a purchase to have:

Ordered
Partially received
Fully received

This handles exceptional supplier delays.

Rule 5 — Damaged material

If the entire received package is damaged:

The whole package can be returned.

If only some material is found to be poor quality after some quantity has already been consumed, the consumed quantity remains accounted for and the remaining material can be returned.

Example:

Received: 60 kg
Used:      1 kg
Remaining: 59 kg
           ↓
Quality problem
           ↓
Return 59 kg
4.6.2 Inventory Rules
Rule 6 — Inventory changes only through recorded events

Raw silk should not simply be manually edited from:

100 kg → 80 kg

without a reason.

The reduction should originate from an event such as:

Sale
Production consumption
Outsourcing issue
Return
Wastage
Adjustment
Rule 7 — Wastage is explicitly recorded

When the owner identifies wastage, it is recorded explicitly as:

Wastage

The system must distinguish wastage from WIP and available material.

Rule 8 — Negative inventory should not normally occur

SmartSilk should prevent the system from showing physically impossible stock such as:

Available raw silk = -15 kg

unless an explicitly authorized adjustment mechanism is introduced.

4.6.3 Customer Order Rules
Rule 9 — Orders are weight-based

For the current system, the important quantity for warp is:

Weight in kg

Detailed warp specifications such as length, width, thread count, etc. are intentionally left outside the current domain model.

Rule 10 — Price is manually determined

The owner enters the selling amount and applicable price/kg.

The system records the actual agreed price rather than attempting to predict it.

Rule 11 — Order cannot be freely modified after production begins

Before production, an order may be changed according to business requirements.

Once production has started:

The order cannot simply be modified/cancelled.

The existing commitment must be respected.

Rule 12 — No partial product delivery

For the current business process, a customer buying multiple warp types cannot receive arbitrary partial quantities of a single warp requirement.

The order is handled according to the agreed warp units/types.

4.6.4 Customer Payment Rules
Rule 13 — Payment reduces customer outstanding

A customer payment must affect the customer's outstanding balance.

Rule 14 — Oldest-first allocation

Unless a future explicit override is introduced:

Customer payments are allocated to the oldest outstanding order first.

Rule 15 — One payment can settle multiple orders

If the payment exceeds one order's outstanding amount, the remainder continues to the next oldest order.

Rule 16 — Excess payment

There should be no uncontrolled excess amount.

Any genuine excess must be handled according to the business process, such as:

Advance for another order
Outstanding adjustment
Refund
4.6.5 Supplier Payment Rules
Rule 17 — Supplier credit period is supplier-specific

Each supplier can have their own credit period.

Rule 18 — Supplier payment is oldest-first

Supplier payments settle the oldest outstanding supplier obligation first.

Rule 19 — Supplier overdue rules are configurable

Any applicable overdue/penalty behaviour must not be hard-coded globally.

4.6.6 Production Rules
Rule 20 — Production consumes material

When raw silk is used for production, available raw silk must decrease and the corresponding WIP must increase.

Available Raw Silk
       ↓
Production Consumption
       ↓
WIP
Rule 21 — Completed production becomes finished inventory
WIP
 ↓
Production completed
 ↓
Finished Warp
Rule 22 — Production weight is the primary measurement

Production and costing primarily use:

kg

4.6.7 Outsourcing Rules
Rule 23 — Outsourced material is tracked separately

When raw silk is given to an external manufacturer:

Available Raw Silk
        ↓
Outsourced WIP

It should not remain counted as freely available stock.

Rule 24 — Returned warp is recorded by weight
Raw silk issued
       ↓
Warp returned
       ↓
Weight recorded
Rule 25 — Vuda warp is direct trading

Vuda warp does not go through the production/outsourcing flow.

Purchase
   ↓
Inventory
   ↓
Sale
4.6.8 Financial Rules
Rule 26 — Every money movement has a classification

Money must have a business meaning such as:

Customer payment
Supplier payment
Worker payment
Expense
Loan
Chit
Refund
Personal drawing
Other appropriate transaction
Rule 27 — Loan principal is not an expense
Loan received → Liability
Principal repayment → Liability reduction
Interest → Expense
Rule 28 — Chit is an investment

Chit contributions and lump-sum receipts are tracked separately from normal sales revenue.

The chit lump-sum receipt is treated as profit/investment return according to the business rule you specified, with the chit calculation handled separately.

Rule 29 — Chit payments are cash-only

For chit transactions:

Only physical cash is permitted.

No UPI, bank transfer, or cheque.

4.6.9 Cash & Bank Rules

SmartSilk must maintain separate balances for:

Physical Cash
Bank Account A
Bank Account B
Bank Account C
...

and also provide:

Total available money
= Physical Cash + Bank balances

But the individual balances must remain visible.

Rule 30 — Physical cash is real inventory of money

Cash withdrawn from a bank increases physical cash.

Bank ↓
Cash ↑

Cash used for an expense/payment:

Cash ↓

The system must therefore allow the owner to understand where physical cash currently came from and where it went.

4.6.10 Financial Analysis Rules
Rule 31 — Revenue ≠ every incoming transaction

For example:

Customer sale → Revenue
Loan → Not revenue
Bank transfer between own accounts → Not revenue
Rule 32 — Personal drawings are separated for analysis

Money taken for personal use should not distort the calculated business profitability.

Rule 33 — Profitability is derived from recorded transactions

The system should eventually calculate:

Monthly profit/loss
Yearly profit/loss
Current expenses
In-house production profitability
Outsourcing profitability
Direct trading profitability

rather than relying on manual estimates.
