Step 5 — MVP Scope and Feature Prioritization

Now we decide what SmartSilk will actually contain in the first usable version.

This is extremely important because your project has many possible features:

Inventory
Purchases
Customer orders
Production
Outsourcing
Payments
Expenses
Loans
Chits
WhatsApp
Voice AI
Financial analytics
Notifications
Reports
Authentication
Dashboards

If we try to build everything at once, the project will become incomplete and unreliable.

The goal is:

Build a small but complete business system that can be genuinely used, rather than a large system with half-working modules.

5.1 What Is an MVP?

MVP means:

Minimum Viable Product

It is not the simplest-looking version.

It is the smallest version that can complete the most important real business workflows from beginning to end.

For SmartSilk, an MVP should allow the owner to answer:

What material did we purchase?
How much material actually arrived?
How much raw silk is available?
What orders are pending?
How much material is outside with manufacturers?
Who owes us money?
Whom do we owe money?
Where did money move?
What expenses occurred?
What work is pending for workers?
What is our current financial position?
5.2 MVP Selection Principle

Every feature should be evaluated using four questions:

1. Business importance

Does the owner genuinely need it?

2. Dependency importance

Do other features depend on it?

3. Implementation effort

How much time and complexity does it require?

4. Risk

Can it create incorrect financial or inventory data?

A feature with high business importance and high dependency should be implemented first.

5.3 Feature Priority Categories

We will use four categories.

P0 — Mandatory Core

Without these, SmartSilk is not useful.

P1 — Important MVP

Strongly recommended for the first usable release.

P2 — Post-MVP

Useful, but not necessary for the first month.

P3 — Future/Experimental

Advanced features that should not threaten the core system.

Module 1: Party Management

Must include:

Add customer
Edit customer
View customer
Add supplier
Edit supplier
Add manufacturer
Add worker
Search parties
Activate/deactivate party

Why mandatory?

Every other module depends on customers, suppliers, manufacturers, or workers.

Module 2: Purchase Management

Must include:

Create purchase
Select supplier
Enter purchase date
Enter ordered quantity
Enter rate/kg
Select cash or credit
Set credit period
Record material receipt
Support partial receipt
Record supplier return
View purchase history
View pending supplier payable

Why mandatory?

Purchasing is the starting point of the material flow.

Module 3: Inventory and Material Tracking

Must include:

Record material receipt
Record material consumption
Record direct raw-silk sale
Record material sent to manufacturer
Record returned material
Record wastage
View available raw silk
View finished warp stock
View external WIP
View internal WIP
Prevent negative stock

Why mandatory?

Inventory is the central operational truth of SmartSilk.

Module 4: Customer Orders

Must include:

Create customer order
Select customer
Add order item
Product type
Required weight
Warp specification
Rate
Expected delivery date
Order status
View pending orders
Mark order ready
Mark order delivered
Restrict changes after production starts

Why mandatory?

The owner currently makes decisions based on incoming orders and available stock.

Module 5: Production

For MVP, support basic in-house production:

Create production batch
Select product type
Link order optionally
Record input weight
Record output weight
Record wastage
Track production status
Record worker work
Move consumed raw silk into WIP
Move completed output into finished stock

Why mandatory?

Without production tracking, SmartSilk cannot represent the core warp business.

Module 6: Outsourcing

Must include:

Create outsourcing job
Select manufacturer
Record material sent
Record date sent
Record quantity sent
Link order optionally
Record received warp
Record wastage/difference
View material currently outside
View manufacturer payable
Mark job completed

Why mandatory?

Outsourcing is a major real-world process and external WIP visibility is one of the most valuable SmartSilk features.

Module 7: Payments and Financial Transactions

Must include:

Record customer payment
Record supplier payment
Record manufacturer payment
Record worker payment
Record expense payment
Record cash transaction
Record bank transaction
Record bank account separately
Record payment method
Allocate payment to obligations
View cash balance
View bank balances
View transaction history

Why mandatory?

A system that tracks material but not money will not solve the owner’s visibility problem.

Module 8: Expenses

Must include:

Add expense
Expense category
Actual payment date
Payment method
Account used
Business/personal classification
Monthly/one-time classification
Expense history

Why mandatory?

The business uses money for many purposes beyond purchases and customer payments.

Module 9: Basic Dashboard

Must display:

Available cash
Bank balances
Total customer outstanding
Total supplier outstanding
Raw silk available
Finished warp stock
Material outside with manufacturers
Pending customer orders
Pending production
Pending outsourcing jobs
Upcoming supplier dues
Recent transactions

Why mandatory?

The dashboard is the main reason the owner would use SmartSilk daily.

5.6 P2 — Post-MVP Features

These are valuable but should not be part of the first critical implementation.

Advanced Profit and Loss
Monthly P&L
Yearly P&L
Product-wise profitability
Customer-wise profitability
Manufacturer-wise profitability
Actual vs estimated margin
Cost allocation

Why postpone?

Profitability becomes inaccurate if the underlying transactions and cost allocation are not mature.

Advanced Inventory Costing
FIFO costing
Weighted average costing
Lot-level traceability
Purchase-lot profitability
Material aging

The current MVP can track quantity and historical purchase prices without implementing sophisticated costing.

Advanced Production Analytics
Worker efficiency
Production per hour
Wastage percentage trends
Manufacturer comparison
Internal vs outsourced production cost
Production capacity forecasting
Advanced Loan and Chit Management
Detailed repayment schedules
Interest calculation
Chit bidding calculation
Chit maturity forecasting
Loan amortization
Automatic interest computation

For MVP, manual entry and transaction history are safer.

Advanced Notifications
WhatsApp reminders
Automated scheduled messages
Notification escalation
Multiple reminder policies
Custom notification rules
5.7 P3 — Future / Experimental Features

These should definitely not block the MVP.

Voice AI

Possible future capabilities:

“Record a ₹5,000 customer payment from Kumar”
“How much silk is currently outside?”
“Which suppliers are overdue?”
“Create an order for 20 kg kora warp”
“What is today’s cash position?”

But voice AI should initially be read-only or confirmation-based.

Never allow voice input to directly mutate financial records without confirmation.

WhatsApp Integration

Future features:

Send invoices
Payment reminders
Order updates
Supplier reminders
Customer balance messages
AI Financial Advisory

Examples:

“Why is cash low despite high sales?”
“Which customers delay payments?”
“Should this job be outsourced?”
“Which expenses increased this month?”
“How much money is committed to suppliers?”

This requires reliable historical data first.

Predictive Analytics
Demand forecasting
Purchase prediction
Cash-flow forecasting
Supplier delay prediction
Production completion prediction

These should come only after enough historical data exists.

5.11 MVP Definition Statement

The official MVP definition can be:

SmartSilk MVP is a modular business visibility system that records purchases, material receipts, material movements, customer orders, internal production, outsourcing, payments, expenses, and financial accounts, and derives current inventory, outstanding balances, external WIP, pending commitments, and cash/bank position through a unified dashboard.

This is a strong and realistic scope.

5.12 MVP Success Criteria

The MVP is considered successful if the owner can perform these tasks without using separate notebooks:

Record a purchase.
Record full or partial receipt.
Return damaged/poor-quality material.
View available raw silk.
Create a customer order.
Produce warp internally.
Send silk to an external manufacturer.
Record received outsourced warp.
See silk currently outside.
Record customer payment.
See customer outstanding.
Record supplier payment.
See supplier payable.
Record expenses.
See cash and bank balances.
See pending orders and commitments.
Review recent business activity from one dashboard.

If these work correctly, the project has achieved its primary purpose.
